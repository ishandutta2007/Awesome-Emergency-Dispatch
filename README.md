## 📊 Market Overview & Industry Structure

> **Market Size & Valuation**: The global Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) market is estimated at **$4.2 Billion to $7.1 Billion USD**, with a projected compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of **10.5% – 12.2%**.  
> **Market Fragmentation**: The sector is **moderately fragmented**, dominated by legacy public safety giants (Motorola Solutions, Hexagon, Tyler Technologies) holding consolidated market share in enterprise 911 PSAPs, alongside fast-growing cloud-native disruptive ECaaS vendors (Mark43, Carbyne, RapidDeploy) capturing next-generation emergency dispatch demand.
